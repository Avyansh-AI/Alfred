# Relatives In Ayodhya

Mama and Mausaji are in the house behind the temple road, which is a 15 minute auto from the new flat - close enough to help, far enough to keep our own routine. Mausaji has already promised to handle the landlord conversation if anything gets sticky, since he knows half the mohalla.

Cousin Renu is a nurse at the RML hospital and she is the reason I am not too worried about Papa's follow-ups. She has offered to get us the physiotherapy slot list and says the morning OPD is the one to aim for.

People to call once we land: Mausaji (already told him the 30th), Renu about the hospital file, and Chachi who will otherwise hear about the move from someone else and be upset for a month.

What they expect from us: sweets from Ambala (see [[Farewell In Ambala]]), the pickle jars Mama asked for, and a Sunday lunch at their place in the first week. I have put that lunch on the [[First Week In Ayodhya]] plan for day 6 evening instead of day 1 because we will be exhausted.
