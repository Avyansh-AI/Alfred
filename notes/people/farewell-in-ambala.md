# Farewell In Ambala

The going-away dinner happens on the 27th at home, and I have been told very firmly that it is not a buffet - Mom is cooking for 22 people. List is basically the neighbours from both floors, my three cousins from Chandigarh side, and Sharmaji who will talk about the traffic the whole evening.

Things to do before everyone arrives: return the two books I borrowed from Anita aunty, give back the drill from the landlord (his, not ours), and settle the milkman for the whole month since he refuses to take part payments.

Gifts we are carrying for the Ayodhya relatives: two boxes of Ambala sweets, the bedsheet set from the last wedding that we never used, and the photo album of Papa's 60th. Mama specifically asked for the pickle jars - those are going in the suitcase, not the truck, and I have accepted that this means one less pair of shoes.

Promised to call everyone from the new place once the internet is up - see [[Internet And Electricity In Ayodhya]].
