# Things To Buy After Moving

Priority one, first 48 hours: a bucket and a mug, two bed sheets, an extension board, a mat for the kitchen, and a working bulb for the bathroom. This is the survival list and it comes out of the 12,000 I set aside in the [[Budget For The Move]].

Priority two, first month: a geyser for the winter bathroom, a small almirah because the flat has no built-in storage, and a water filter - the landlord says the tank water is fine but I am not drinking it unfiltered for a year.

Nice to have, deliberately deferred: a second fan, a bookshelf, and a proper study chair. I have put these on a wishlist and will buy them one per month instead of all at once.

Where to buy: the main market near the temple road for the almirah, and online for the geyser and the filter. Compare the geyser warranty carefully - the cheap one has a two-year tank warranty and the mid one has seven, and we plan to stay in the new flat at least three years.
