# Vande Bharat Train

Booked the Vande Bharat from Ambala to Ayodhya via Delhi for the 30th, chair car, window pair on the left side. Two large suitcases, one cabin bag and my laptop sleeve - that is the entire allowance we are using.

Timings that matter: leave home by 5:10 am for the Ambala Cantt drop, breakfast at the Delhi halt is a 40 minute window so we carry parathas instead of buying, and we land in Ayodhya by late afternoon. Total door-to-door is about 12 hours.

What I want to remember for next time: book chair car on the quieter coach, carry a light shawl because the AC is aggressive, and pre-book the porter at Ayodhya instead of negotiating at the platform. The 40 kg limit is per person and nobody weighed us on the last trip, but I am not counting on that.

Papa is travelling with us, so I asked for lower seats near the door. Full plan in [[Moving To Ayodhya]], packing order in [[Packing Stuff]], and the route options we rejected in [[Route And Halts]].
