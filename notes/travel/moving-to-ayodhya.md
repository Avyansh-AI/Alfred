# Moving To Ayodhya

We finally decided: the shift to Ayodhya is happening the first week of next month. Mom's been saying it for two years and this time the paperwork actually moved.

Why Ayodhya: Papa's knee surgery follow-ups are at the Ram Manohar Lohia hospital, and my new role is remote so I can work from anywhere as long as the internet holds up. Rent here in Ambala has crept up to 14,000 for a place we have outgrown anyway.

Plan so far: pack up in three phases (see [[Packing Stuff]]), hand over the keys on the 30th, and take the [[Vande Bharat Train]] up with the two big suitcases while the movers take everything else by road. The [[Budget For The Move]] is roughly 55,000 including the mover, the deposit and the first month.

Open worries: the flat we liked near Devangana is still not confirmed, and I have not yet figured out whether we keep the Ambala internet connection or start fresh. Everything else is on the [[Moving Day Checklist]].
