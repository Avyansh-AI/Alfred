# Route And Halts

Three ways to get from Ambala to Ayodhya and why we picked the train:
1. Road, the full 900 km in one go: cheapest on paper but the mover does this anyway with our boxes, so we would be doing it twice.
2. Ambala to Lucknow by train, halt, then Lucknow to Ayodhya the next morning: two hotel nights, and honestly too much luggage handling.
3. Ambala to Delhi, change, Delhi to Ayodhya on the [[Vande Bharat Train]]: one change, one long leg, arrives the same evening.

The halt we did consider: a night in Delhi with my cousin so we could drop the heavy bags and travel light the next morning. Decided against it because Papa's medicines need a clean fridge space and we cannot carry insulin in a hotel.

Road distance for the movers is roughly 900 km on the Lucknow route and they quoted for two days including a night halt for the driver. Compare against the [[Budget For The Move]] before approving anything extra.
