# Internet And Electricity In Ayodhya

Internet: the flat has no existing fibre line, so I have to book a new connection. Two operators service the lane - a Jio fibre reseller and an Airtel authorised guy who quoted 4,999 for installation with 100 Mbps and no monthly for three months. The Jio reseller quoted 3,500 with a 30 Mbps plan. I want the 100 Mbps because I take client calls all day and my phone hotspot is not going to survive a 90 minute call with the screen share on.

Electricity: UPPCL connection is already live, meter is in the landlord's name, so I will pay him the bill each month against the reading. I am photographing the meter on the day we move in and will do it again on the 30th of every month. Tariff is roughly 7 per unit for the slab we are in.

Backup plan: keep the Ambala number for two more months, keep a 4G dongle as a fallback for the first week, and do not cancel the old broadband until the new line is tested with a real call. Notes on the handover in [[Moving Day Checklist]].
