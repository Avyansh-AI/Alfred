# New Flat In Ayodhya

The flat we want: 2BHK, second floor, near the Devangana crossing, 9,500 a month with a 20,000 deposit. Landlord is a retired teacher who wants a one-year agreement and does not want families with pets - we have no pets so this is fine.

What sold me: a proper balcony, the water tank on the roof (no dependence on the municipal line in summer), and the fact that the RML hospital is a 12 minute auto ride. The kitchen is small but has a window, which the other flat did not.

Problems: the flat needs a geyser and the wiring is old, so I have budgeted for a stabiliser. The bathroom tap leaks and the landlord says the plumber will come in the first week. I want that in the agreement or it will never happen.

Decisions still open: do we take the flat for 11 months and renegotiate, or sign the full year at 9,500 flat? Notes in [[Budget For The Move]] and [[First Week In Ayodhya]].
